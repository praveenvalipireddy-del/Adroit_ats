🎓 US Bench Sourcing: India Bachelor's (≤2020) + USA Master's

Recruiter Pipeline: Engineers who completed Bachelor's in India in that EXACT year (e.g. 2020) (4–10+ yrs tech experience) and pursued Master's in the USA (OPT / STEM OPT / H1B)

How it works: "Search LinkedIn" below scans real LinkedIn profiles of people in the USA who studied at Indian colleges (JNTU, Osmania, VIT, SRM, Manipal, Amrita, Anna, VTU, BITS, IITs/NITs and more) and reads each profile's Education section. A profile is shown only if its Bachelor's is from an Indian college and ended in exactly the year you select, and it lists a Master's from a non-Indian school. Profiles that don't list a year are skipped, never guessed.
